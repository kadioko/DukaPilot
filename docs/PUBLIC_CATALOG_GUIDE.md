# Public Catalog Guide

Shop owners can publish a shop catalog and control visibility product by product.

## Share the shop link

1. Open **Inventory** and choose **Share catalog**, or open **Settings > Shop Details**.
2. Turn on **Publish shop in the public catalog** and save the shop settings.
3. Copy or share the ordering link, open the public shop, or download its QR code.

The shop-wide switch controls whether the public catalog and ordering are available. When it is off, the public shop is unavailable even if individual products are visible.

## Hide an individual product

When adding or editing a product, leave **Show this product in the public catalog** on to list it to customers. Turn it off to keep the product out of the public catalog while retaining it in internal inventory and POS workflows. Owners can also use **Inventory > product actions > Hide from catalog** for a quick change. Hidden products cannot be ordered through an already-open catalog page.

Visibility is separate from stock and product activation. Public listings still omit inactive and out-of-stock products. New products default to visible to preserve the previous catalog behavior; review visibility before sharing a newly prepared catalog.

## Troubleshooting

- If the link says the shop is unavailable, confirm the shop is published, active, and on an active trial or subscription.
- If a product is missing, check its catalog visibility, active status, and available stock.
- Changes take effect on the next catalog request; a customer may need to refresh an already-open page.
